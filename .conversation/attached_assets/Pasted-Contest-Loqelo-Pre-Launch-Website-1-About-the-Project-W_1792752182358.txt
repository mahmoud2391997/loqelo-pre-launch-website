Contest: Loqelo Pre-Launch Website
1. About the Project
We are looking for a creative developer to design and implement a modern pre-launch website for Loqelo—a platform that connects customers with service providers for the home, garden, and office.
Loqelo's planned launch: February 2027.
The purpose of the website is to present the Loqelo concept, generate interest ahead of the launch, and collect email addresses from future customers and contractors in various countries.
We want an attractive design, ease of use, and an effective sign-up form. We don’t need a complex, custom-programmed system—we’d be happy to use simple, proven solutions.

2. What do we offer?
We’ll provide materials to make your job easier:
• the finished Loqelo logo and visual identity;
• UI/UX designs for the platform in Figma files;
• a promotional video for Loqelo.
You don’t have to design the brand or the platform’s interface from scratch. You can focus on creating an attractive website that makes use of the existing materials.

3. What should the website look like?
We want a modern, minimalist, and professional landing page in the style of a global tech startup.
The page should include:
• A main section with the logo and the tagline “Everything your place needs.”
• A promotional video and an attractive presentation of selected platform screenshots.
• A brief explanation of how Loqelo works—from the customer describing their need, through matching them with service providers, to receiving offers and selecting a provider.
• Information about the planned launch in February 2027
• Waitlist sign-up forms for two groups: customers and service providers.
• Clear calls to action encouraging users to sign up before the launch.
We’re giving you the freedom to decide on the layout, animations, transitions, and presentation style. The most important thing is that the page is visually appealing and easy to understand.

4. Forms and Waiting List
We want to collect contact information from two groups of users.
Customers:
• email address;
• country of residence;
• optional: individual or business.
Contractors:
• email address;
• country where they conduct business or provide services;
• optional: business or individual.
After signing up, the user will see a brief confirmation message.
We also require an appropriate mechanism for obtaining consent to receive messages regarding project launches and updates.
We do not expect you to build your own email marketing system. You may use a ready-made tool for collecting and managing contacts, provided that it allows you to distinguish between clients and contractors and export data to CSV.

5. Basic Statistics and Analytics
We would like to see if the website is generating interest and from which countries potential users are coming.
Please implement a simple analytics solution, such as Google Analytics 4, and configure basic events, if the chosen solution allows for it.
We want to be able to track:
• the number of visitors;
• countries;
• the number of customer and contractor sign-ups;
• the effectiveness of forms and the basic conversion rate.
Analytics should comply with required consents and privacy policies. Email addresses and form content must not be shared with analytics tools.

6. Technology and Requirements
To keep the project within a reasonable budget, you can use a ready-made template, a CMS, a website builder, or another lightweight solution. We don't require you to build everything from scratch.
The website should:
• function properly on computers and mobile devices;
• load quickly;
• be translated into the user’s language once their location is detected, and allow them to easily change the language;
• have basic SEO optimization;
• include secure forms and spam protection;
• allow for easy content editing.
We provide the [loqelo.com](https://www.freelancer.com/users/l.php?url=http:%2F%2Floqelo.com&sig=c624d65cf30dd348b059dc305414850a8bb655309a28d61b9a594214c08f52f5) domain, hosting, and email accounts. The contractor can assist with their setup.

7. What should be communicated at the end?
We expect a fully functional website that's ready to go live, not just a graphic design in Figma.
As part of the agreed scope, we would like to receive:
• a deployed and tested website;
• functional forms and the ability to manage contacts;
• basic analytics configuration;
• administrative access to the website and the tools used;
• source files or the ability to manage the deployment ourselves;
• a brief user manual.
Please indicate whether the proposed solution requires additional paid licenses or subscriptions. We prefer tools that are free or have low maintenance costs.

8. How will we choose the winner?
The most important factors for us will be:
1. An attractive, modern design.
2. A clear presentation of the Loqelo concept.
3. A good user experience and an inviting sign-up form.
4. Proper functionality on mobile devices (responsiveness).
5. A simple and reliable technical solution.
6. The ability to easily manage contacts and analyze basic statistics.
We are looking for a contractor who can combine good visual design with practical implementation.
Thank you for your interest in the Loqelo project, and we look forward to receiving your creative ideas!
